Hi, I'm Torie.

I make earrings at my kitchen table. Rolling clay, stringing beads, painting shells I picked up off the beach. I started because I wanted to wear something nobody else had.

Every pair is cut, shaped and finished by hand, so small differences are not mistakes. They are the proof a person made it. No templates, no factory, and nothing here was made by a machine pretending to be me.
